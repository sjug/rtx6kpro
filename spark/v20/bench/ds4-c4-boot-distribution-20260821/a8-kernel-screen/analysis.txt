All outputs were finite and both paths passed the B12X oracle threshold of
cosine >= 0.998 with norm ratios inside 0.98..1.02. The dynamic fallback is
closer to the oracle at every tested tiny M, but it is materially slower at
M=1 and M=3. The stock tiny kernel remains the correct broad policy. M=4 is a
narrow unresolved opportunity: routing only that exact shape to dynamic saves
about 22 us per MoE invocation in this synthetic screen, but it requires a
source-level selector and a two-node serving A/B to establish that M=4 occurs
often enough to matter.

Verdict: reject persistent_grid, disabling the materialized intermediate,
disabling shared-input reuse, and globally disabling tiny decode. Preserve the
stock W4A8 policy. Keep an M=4-only dynamic selector as the sole positive
microbenchmark lead; it is not yet a release candidate.
